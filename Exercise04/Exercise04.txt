Ex4 Part01
#pragma omp parallel for distributes iterations of the outer loop, which is r, to all threads available. As r loop contains 5 iterations, hence those 5 iterations will be distributed to all threads. After receiving one iteration of r, a particular thread executes all 10 iterations of inner loop k.

Ex4 Part02
The function collapse(2) merges both of these nested loops into one iteration space. Here we have total iterations of 5 * 10 = 50, and these 50 iterations can be divided among the threads. It usually results in efficient workload division compared to parallelization of just the 5 outer iterations alone.
